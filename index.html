Before you get in touch

Are you an Odoo partner?

No. We are an independent engineering team. We work in Odoo every day, and we use other tools when they fit the problem better.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a free first call you get a written proposal with a fixed price, so you know the total before anything starts.

How long does it take?

A focused automation can be ready in days; a full system takes weeks. The proposal includes a timeline with clear milestones.

Can you work on the system we already have?

Yes. Often the fastest result comes from improving, connecting or automating what you already use.

Is AI safe to use with our data?

We give each agent access only to what its job needs, keep a log of every action, and let a person approve anything that matters.

Do you build websites in Arabic?

Yes, in Arabic and English with a proper right-to-left layout, connected to WhatsApp and your other systems.
